# Armillary Phase 3: Stripe

Status: built and reviewed 2026-09-20, version 4 on both ships (api-matrix 101, ship-matrix 63 in both shapes, unit tests 67 + 9 + 14). The `success_url` line below is corrected to what Stripe accepts.

**Goal:** the card rail. A customer's `checkout` op in live mode makes a Stripe Checkout Session for a top-up or a subscription, the webhook and the return page verify the payment by reading it back from Stripe and credit the ship's account, a subscription credits its plan every period, and the vendor page's Payments view holds the settings and the plans. Built on phase 2's head (`047a0a7` and the docs commits after it). Spec sections 3 (plans), 6 (checkout), 7 (Stripe), 8 and 9 are the requirements; the phase 1 brief's recipes, constraints and file structure hold; phase 2's channel is what carries the customer's side. Read the spec sections, both earlier briefs' Working rules and Global constraints, and `~/software/personal/register/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-17-register-phase-2-providers.md` section 1 and the Pay and webhook paragraphs of section 2, which are the same integration one desk over.

## 2. Settings, plans, the view

Settings gain `stripe_key`, `stripe_webhook_secret` and `stripe_url` (default `https://api.stripe.com`, the stub overrides it); `+$ settings` grows; `mask-doc` masks the two secrets; `set-settings` keeps a stored secret when the incoming one is blank and clears it on `null`, the provider rule; the two never appear on any read route unmasked, in the ring, or in the view. `min_topup` (5.000.000) and `public_url` already exist.

`plans.json` becomes a map by id of `+$ plan` `[id=@t name=@t kind=?(%topup %subscription) price=@ud credit=@ud interval=@t stripe-price=@t]`; `de-plan` names the failing field (`id: 1 to 64 bytes`, `kind: topup or subscription`, `price: above zero`, `credit: above zero`, `interval: month or year` for a subscription), `en-plan`, `en-plans-public` (every field but nothing secret; `stripe_price` is not a secret). Writer ops `set-plan`, `drop-plan` (refused 409 by the route when an open subscription names it: walk the accounts). Owner routes `GET /api/plans`, `POST /api/plans` (409 when the id exists), `PUT /api/plans/<id>`, `DELETE /api/plans/<id>`, `POST /api/plans/<id>/stripe` (`product-request` then `price-request` through `+fetch`, store `stripe_price`, answer the plan; 502 with Stripe's `error.message` on failure; 400 `stripe_key: not set`). `plans.json` is already peekable by `/public` from phase 2, so the customer's `GET /api/plans` (new, on the customer ship: the local map when the vendor is us, else `peek-remote-wait` on `plans.json`, 502 on `~`) needs no new grant.

The account row gains `plan=@t`, `stripe-customer=@t`, `stripe-subscription=@t`, `renews=(unit @da)`; `en-account`, `de-account`, the summary and the owner detail carry `plan` and `subscription` (`{"active": <bool>, "renews": <iso or null>, "id": <stripe id>}`), never the customer id on a customer-facing view (the view's `subscription` is `{active, renews}` only). Writer op `set-subscription` (`ship`, `customer`, `subscription`, `plan`, `renews`) and `clear-subscription` (`ship`). The view's `plan` and `subscription` are filled from the account row.

## 3. Checkout, live

`+inbox-checkout`'s live branch (`settings.mode` is `live`), for `rail` `stripe` only (`btcpay` stays `unavailable` until phase 4): 400-class refusals are `set-checkout` rows with `status` `refused` and `note` naming the reason, since the customer only sees the view: `stripe_key: not set`, `plan: unknown`, `amount: below the minimum`, `plan and amount: choose one`. Then `+fetch` the session request from the inbox fiber itself. A Stripe call blocks the inbox for up to two minutes, which serializes every customer's ops behind one slow call: mark that ceiling with a `ponytail:` comment naming a spawned per-op fiber as the upgrade. The request: a top-up (`amount` set, or a `topup` plan whose `price` is the amount) with `label` `Armillary credit` and `success` `<public_url>/apps/armillary/pay/return?ship=<ship>` and `cancel` the same with `&cancelled=1`, `expires` now plus 24 hours in unix seconds; a subscription (`plan` of kind `subscription`, `stripe_price` set, else `refused` `plan: not on Stripe yet`) with `subscription-request`. On a 2xx, `read-session`; `set-checkout` with `url`, `status` `pending`, `sid` the session id, `expires`; on anything else `status` `refused`, `note` `stripe answered <status>: <error.message>`, no secret. The customer's `POST /api/checkout` on its own ship answers the `url` when the row's status is `pending`, and 502 `{"error": {"message": <note>}}` when `refused`.

There is no bearer checkout route over HTTP. The account channel is ames only; Talon uses its ship.

## 4. Verification: the webhook and the return page

`+credit-session |=([sid=@t] (fiber ,[ok=? why=@t]))`, one arm both routes call: `+fetch` the session; `read-session`; refuse when not paid or when `ship` is not an `@p`; find the checkout row on that ship by `sid` in `checkouts.json` (the row is written before the URL is answered, so a missing row means a session this ship never made: refuse `unknown session`); credit `total * 10.000` microdollars with `rail` `stripe`, `ref` the session id (deduped by the credit op; a repeat answers ok `already recorded`); for `mode` `subscription`, `set-subscription` with `customer`, `subscription`, the row's `plan`, `renews` unknown yet (`~`); set the checkout row `status` `paid`; the view is rewritten by the credit op.

`+credit-invoice |=([iid=@t] (fiber ,[ok=? why=@t]))`: `+fetch` the invoice; `read-invoice`; refuse when not paid; find the account whose `stripe-customer` is `customer` (walk `accounts`); find the plan whose `stripe-price` is `price` (else the account's `plan`); credit the plan's `credit` with `ref` the invoice id; `set-subscription` with `renews` from `period-end`.

`POST /hooks/stripe`, public, no cookie: when `stripe_webhook_secret` is set, `verify-signature` on the `stripe-signature` header and the raw body with `now`; a bad or missing signature is 400 `signature`. Then `event-of`; `~` answers 200 `{"ok": true}`. `checkout.session.completed` and `checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded`: `credit-session`. `invoice.paid`: `credit-invoice`. `customer.subscription.deleted`: find the account by `stripe-subscription` equal to the id and `clear-subscription`. Everything else 200 and nothing. Answer 200 `{"ok": true}` in every case, including a failed fetch, which is noted in the ring as `stripe.webhook` not ok with the why (Stripe retries non-2xx, and a retry storm on a dead upstream helps nobody). The body is never trusted for anything but the event type and the object id.

`GET /pay/return?ship&sid[&cancelled]`, public: a small HTML page (`return.html`, laid as an `%over` asset like the page files, with `{{state}}` and `{{detail}}` replaced by the handler; `esc()` nothing, since the handler writes only its own fixed strings). With `cancelled`: "Payment cancelled. Go back to the app." With `sid`: `credit-session`; ok: "Payment received. Your balance updates on your ship within a minute. You can close this tab."; not ok: "We have not confirmed this payment yet. Give it a minute and reload." plus the why when it is not a secret. No `sid`: the pending text. The handler never reads a cookie and never shows a balance.

`cancel-subscription` op (inbox): 501 no more; `cancel-request` through `+fetch` on the account's `stripe-subscription`; note the outcome in `/tr/inbox`; the subscription row stays until `customer.subscription.deleted` arrives (or the owner's `clear-subscription`). The customer's `POST /api/cancel-subscription` queues the op and answers 202.

## 5. The stub and the page

`scripts/fake-stripe.py PORT SECRET SHIP_URL`: `http.server`, no dependencies, an in-memory store. `POST /v1/checkout/sessions` (form body; requires Bearer) answers `{"id": "cs_<n>", "url": "http://127.0.0.1:PORT/stub/pay/cs_<n>", "mode", "payment_status": "unpaid", "amount_total": <from unit_amount times quantity>, "metadata": {"ship"}, "customer": null, "subscription": null}`, keeping `subscription_data[metadata][plan]` and the `price` on the record. `GET /v1/checkout/sessions/<id>` answers the record. `GET /stub/pay/<id>` (a browser page) or `POST /stub/pay/<id>` marks it paid: `payment_status` `paid`, and for `mode=subscription` invents `customer` `cus_<n>`, `subscription` `sub_<n>`, an invoice `in_<n>` with `status` `paid`, `lines.data[0].price.id` the price and `period.end` now plus 30 days; then POSTs `checkout.session.completed` and, for a subscription, `invoice.paid` to `SHIP_URL/apps/armillary/hooks/stripe` with a `stripe-signature` header made with SECRET (skipped when SECRET is `-`). `GET /v1/invoices/<id>`. `POST /v1/products` and `POST /v1/prices` answer ids `prod_<n>` and `price_<n>` echoing the fields. `POST /v1/subscriptions/<id>` marks `cancel_at_period_end`. `POST /stub/renew/<sub>` invents the next invoice and POSTs `invoice.paid`. `POST /stub/delete/<sub>` POSTs `customer.subscription.deleted`. `GET /stub/state` dumps the store. Everything else 404 `{"error": {"message": "stub: no such route"}}`.

The page's **Payments** view (`#payments`, vendor only): the Stripe card (key masked with `leave blank to keep`, webhook secret the same, `stripe_url` shown only when it is not the default, the public URL, the webhook URL to paste into Stripe as `<public_url>/apps/armillary/hooks/stripe`), the plans table (id, name, kind, price and credit in dollars, interval, the Stripe price id or a Create on Stripe button, Edit, Delete) with an add form, and the last ten `stripe.*` ring entries. The customer's Account view: the plans as buttons (top-up plans credit their amount, subscription plans say `per month` or `per year`), the custom amount field, and under the balance the subscription line (`Subscribed to <name>, renews <date>` with a Cancel button, or nothing). The owner's account detail shows the subscription with the Stripe ids and a Clear button (`clear-subscription`, for a subscription Stripe says is gone).
