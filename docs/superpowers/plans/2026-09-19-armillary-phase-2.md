# Armillary Phase 2: the account channel over ames

**Goal:** a customer ship manages its account on the vendor over ames: `hello` opens the account, `mint-key` and `got-key` deliver an inference key through a view only that ship can peek, `drop-key` revokes it, and Talon gets everything it needs from `GET /api/inference` on the customer's own ship. Checkout is stubbed; money rails are phases 3 and 4; leases are phase 5. Built on phase 1's head (`bfadedc` and the docs commit after it). The spec is `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-19-armillary-design.md`; sections 2, 6, 8, 9 and 10 are the requirements. The phase 1 brief (`2026-09-19-armillary-phase-1.md`) holds the `~wex` recipes, the global constraints and the file structure; all of it still holds. Read both before any task.

## Task 1: the lib

`code/lib/armillary.hoon` gains, import-free as before:

- `+armillary-instance` the path `/apps/shell.shell/desks/armillary.desk/desk/data/armillary.armillary_app` (as a `path` constant with the knots quoted the way orrery's `orrery-instance` is).
- `+group-name |=(who=@p @t)`: `armillary-` followed by the ship's `@p` text without the sig (`armillary-wex`).
- `+is-comet |=(who=@p ?)`: `?=(%pawn (clan:title who))`.
- Inbox ops: `+$ inbox-op` is a tagged union `[%hello ~]`, `[%refresh ~]`, `[%checkout rail=@t plan=@t amount=@ud nonce=@t]`, `[%mint-key name=@t nonce=@t]`, `[%got-key id=@t]`, `[%drop-key id=@t]`, `[%lease ~]`, `[%drop-lease ~]`, `[%cancel-subscription ~]`. `+de-inbox |=(jon=json (each inbox-op @t))` names the failing field (`op: unknown`, `nonce: 1 to 64 bytes`, `name: 1 to 200 bytes`, `rail: stripe or btcpay`, `plan or amount required`). `+en-inbox |=(inbox-op json)` is the exact inverse; a test round-trips every variant.
- The view: `+$ view` is `[ship=@p balance=@sd plan=@t subscription=json keys=(list json) keys-pending=(list [nonce=@t id=@t name=@t secret=@t]) lease=json checkouts=json ledger=(list json) rev=@ud updated=@da]`. `+en-view` and `+de-view` (`~` on a missing `ship` or `rev`). `keys_pending` on the wire is `[{nonce, id, name, secret}]`. `+view-nonces |=(v=view (set @t))`: every nonce present in `keys_pending` and in `checkouts`, so the customer side can tell whether an op landed.
- Customer-side keys: `+$ held-key` is `[id=@t name=@t secret=@t made=@da]`; `en-held`, `de-held`, `en-held-public` (no secret). `+inference-json |=([mode=@t base=@t key=@t models=(list @t)] json)`.
- Writer ops for the vendor: `de-op-view` (`ship`), `de-op-pending` (`ship`, `id`, `secret`, `nonce`, `name`) and `de-op-drop-pending` (`ship`, `id`), `de-op-checkout` (`ship`, `nonce`, `rail`, `plan`, `amount`, `url`, `expires`, `status`); for the customer: `de-op-vendor` (`ship`), `de-op-store-key` (a `held-key`), `de-op-forget-key` (`id`), `de-op-store-view` (the view as JSON, stored verbatim with `fetched`), `de-op-note-op` (`nonce`, `op`, `sent`) and `de-op-drop-op` (`nonce`).

`tests/lib/armillary.hoon` gains about 15 arms: `de-inbox` on every variant and on `{"op": "x"}`, on a `mint-key` with no nonce, on a `checkout` with neither plan nor amount; the `en-inbox` round trip; `is-comet` on `~wex` (no), `~sampel-palnet-sampel-palnet` (no, a moon) and a comet name (yes); `group-name`; `en-view` then `de-view` round trip; `view-nonces` finds a checkout nonce and a pending key nonce; `inference-json` shape. ## Task 2: the vendor side of the channel

In `code/nex/armillary/app.hoon`:

**The inbox fiber** at `/inbox.sig`, orrery's `shares.sig` handler shape: `rise-wait`, `lay-inbox-road` (orrery's, with `inbox.sig` in place of `shares.sig`), then the loop over `take-poke-from`. The source is `(fall (get-poke-src:io from) our)`: a local poke is this ship acting as its own customer, which the spec allows and the gate uses. Each poke is decoded with `de-inbox`; a refusal is noted in `/tr/inbox` (a ring of 500, like orrery's `note-inbox`; add `tr/inbox` to `on-load`) and nothing else happens. The inbox fiber never writes account state itself: it pokes the writer with the op it decoded plus `ship` (never trusting a `ship` in the payload), waits for nothing, and continues. Exception: `hello` and any op from a ship with no account first go through `open-account`, and when `refuse_comets` is on and `is-comet` says yes, the op is noted `comet refused` and dropped.

**The per-ship group and the view.** `+ensure-group |=(who=@p (fiber ,~))`: `self-base` (orrery's, reading `/sys/link/armillary/dest.lanes`), then `ug-set` (orrery's) with the group name from `group-name`, the ships `{who}`, the peek set holding one road `[%& %& (weld base (acct-dir who)) %'view.json']`, and no poke roads. Called by the writer inside `open-account`, and once more by `write-view` if the group directory does not exist (check with `peek-exists` on its `who.ships`). `+do-write-view` (op `write-view`): read `account.json`, `keys.json`, `pending.json`, `lease.json` (empty in this phase), `checkouts.json`, the newest 50 ledger rows; build the `view` with `rev` one more than the stored view's; write `view.json`. Every writer op that touches an account ends by calling the same arm (not by poking itself), so the view is always current: `open-account`, `credit`, `debit`, `refund`, `add-key`, `drop-key`, `close-account`, `set-pending`, `drop-pending`, `set-checkout`.

**The ops on the vendor**, as spec section 6, each a writer op or a pair:

- `hello`: `open-account` (no-op when open; refused when closed with `account closed`), then `write-view`.
- `refresh`: `write-view`.
- `mint-key`: the inbox fiber mints exactly as `serve-mint` does (entropy, id, salt, secret, hash) and pokes `add-key` with the hashed row plus `set-pending` with `{ship, nonce, id, name, secret}`; the writer refuses over 20 keys and then also refuses the pending row. The secret is in `pending.json` and in the view's `keys_pending` and nowhere else; never in the ring. A `mint-key` whose nonce is already in `pending.json` is a no-op (idempotent).
- `got-key`: `drop-pending` for that id.
- `drop-key`: `drop-key` (the existing op) with the source ship; a key on another account cannot be named because the op is scoped to the poking ship's `keys.json`.
- `checkout`: in this phase only the stub: when `settings.mode` is `stub`, `set-checkout` with `url` `<public_url or http://localhost:8080>/apps/armillary/pay/stub?ship=<ship>&nonce=<nonce>` and `status` `pending`, `expires` now plus 24 hours; when `live`, `set-checkout` with `status` `unavailable` and an empty url (phase 3 replaces this branch). A repeated nonce answers the stored row.
- `lease`, `drop-lease`, `cancel-subscription`: noted in `/tr/inbox` as `not yet` and dropped. Phases 3 and 5.

**The stub pay page**, owner or public? Public: `GET /pay/stub?ship&nonce` answers a small HTML page with one button that POSTs `/pay/stub` (public) with the same two fields; the handler, in stub mode only (403 `stub mode only` otherwise), credits the checkout's `amount` (or the plan's `credit`; plans are phase 3, so `amount` only, and a `plan` checkout in stub mode credits 10.000.000) with rail `stub` and `ref` `stub-<nonce>` (deduped by the credit op, so a double click credits once), sets the checkout `status` `paid`, and answers `paid`. This is what lets the whole loop run on `~wex` with no money.

**Owner routes touched**: `GET /api/accounts/<ship>` also shows `pending` (ids and names, never secrets) and `checkouts`. `DELETE /api/accounts/<ship>` (the gate's hard delete) also drops the group (`ug-set` with an empty ship set) and `tr/inbox` entries stay.

Commit: `The vendor side of the account channel: the inbox, the per-ship view and the stub pay page`.

## Task 3: the customer side and Talon's routes

**The client fiber** at `/client.sig`, orrery's `sync.sig` shape: `rise-wait`, then a loop: read `vendor.json`; when it is empty, wait for a poke; else run `client-pass` (below), set a five minute timer, `take-poke-from` (a poke from a request fiber, or the timer), cancel the timer, loop. A poke carries `{"send": <inbox op json>}` or `{"peek": true}`. `client-pass` sends every op in `client.json` not yet sent (poke the vendor's inbox), then peeks the view once and stores it.

`+send-op |=([vendor=@p jon=json] (fiber ,?))`: when `vendor` is `our`, `poke-soft:io` on `(rf 0 / %'inbox.sig')` and answer yes; else `remote-poke-wait` (orrery's, verbatim, with the lane `[%& armillary-instance %'inbox.sig']`). `+peek-view |=(vendor=@p (fiber ,(unit json)))`: when `vendor` is `our`, `read-json` on our own `/accounts/<our>/view.json`; else `peek-remote-wait` (orrery's, verbatim) on `[%& %& (weld armillary-instance (acct-dir our)) %'view.json']` and the sang decoded as JSON. Store it with `store-view`, then for every `keys_pending` row whose nonce is in `client.json`'s ops: `store-key` and send `got-key`, and `drop-op` the nonce. A checkout nonce seen in the view's `checkouts` also `drop-op`s.

**Talon's routes** on the customer ship, owner cookie, spec section 8's last table, in this phase:

- `GET /api/account`: the stored view plus `vendor` and `stale` (seconds since `fetched`); `?fresh=1` pokes `client.sig` with `peek` and waits up to 30 seconds for `fetched` to move, then answers what is stored.
- `PUT /api/vendor {"ship"}`: `set-vendor`, then queue `hello` and prod `client.sig`. `{"ship": ""}` clears it.
- `POST /api/keys {"name"}`: queue `mint-key` with a fresh nonce, prod, wait up to 30 seconds for the key to land in `keys.json` (poll the file every second), answer `{"id", "name", "secret"}` or 202 `{"pending": true, "nonce"}`.
- `GET /api/keys`: `en-held-public` rows. `DELETE /api/keys/<id>`: queue `drop-key`, `forget-key` at once, answer ok.
- `POST /api/checkout {"rail", "plan" or "amount"}`: queue `checkout` with a nonce, prod, wait for the nonce in the view's `checkouts`, answer `{"url"}` or 202 `{"pending": true, "nonce"}`.
- `GET /api/inference`: `{"mode": "proxy", "base_url": "<vendor public URL or http://localhost:8080>/apps/armillary/v1", "key": "<id>.<secret>" of the newest held key, "models": [ids from the vendor's public catalog]}`; 404 `no key yet` when no key is held. The vendor's public URL comes from `view.json` (add `public_url` to the view in Task 2 from settings, empty in stub mode means `http://localhost:8080`). Lease mode is phase 5.
- `GET /api/catalog`: the vendor's `catalog-public.json` by the same peek helper (local read when the vendor is us; remote `peek-remote-wait` on `[%& %& armillary-instance %'catalog-public.json']` otherwise, which needs the vendor to grant `/public` a peek on that file: add to `lay-inbox-road` a second registry line granting `/public` peek on `catalog-public.json` and `plans.json`). Answered live, never cached; 502 `vendor unreachable` on `~`.
- `POST /api/cancel-subscription`, `POST /api/lease`, `DELETE /api/lease`: 501 `not yet` in this phase.

**The page**, customer views (`#account`, `#keys`, `#catalog`) from spec section 9, shown when `vendor.json` names a ship other than our own, and a "This ship is its own customer" note plus the same views when it names our own ship (the gate's case); the vendor views stay when the vendor is empty or ourselves. Account: vendor, balance, the ledger, a top-up form (amount in dollars, rail radio card or bitcoin) that opens the checkout URL in a new tab, a Set vendor field. Keys: mint (secret shown once), the held keys with revoke, the inference config as `GET /api/inference` shows it with a copy button. Catalog: the vendor's models and prices.
