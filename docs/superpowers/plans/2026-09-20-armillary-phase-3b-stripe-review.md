## 1. Credit the subtotal

`+read-session` gains `subtotal=@ud` from `amount_subtotal` and `intent=@t` from `payment_intent` (a string or null, `''` when null). `+credit-session` credits `subtotal * 10.000`, never `amount_total`, so a session that one day carries tax credits the goods and not the tax. The paid fixture in `tests/lib/armillary-stripe.hoon` gains `amount_subtotal` and `payment_intent` and the test asserts both; the stub answers `amount_subtotal` equal to `amount_total` and a `payment_intent` `pi_<n>` on a paid session. `+mark-checkout` stores `intent` on the row when paid (section 4 needs it).

## 2. The signing secret is required in live mode

`+do-set-settings` refuses `mode` `live` when `stripe_key` is set and `stripe_webhook_secret` would be blank after the save, with `stripe_webhook_secret: required in live mode`; the route answers 400 with that text. `+serve-stripe-hook` in live mode with a blank secret answers 400 `signature: no signing secret configured` and processes nothing, with a `stripe.webhook` ring row; in stub mode a blank secret still skips the check, since the stub is local. The Payments view says beside the field that live mode needs it. `docs/payments.md`'s trust rule paragraph is rewritten: the signature is checked always in live mode, and the read-back is defense in depth, not the only trust.

## 3. One Customer per ship

`armillary-stripe.hoon` gains `+customer-request |=([base=@t key=@t ship=@t] request:http)`: POST `/v1/customers` with `metadata[ship]` and `description` `Armillary <ship>`; `+read-id` already reads the answer. `+topup-request` and `+subscription-request` gain `customer=@t` and send `customer=<id>` when it is not blank (a top-up with a customer also sends `customer_update[name]=auto` so Checkout may fill the name it collects). The writer gains `set-stripe-customer` (`ship`, `customer`), decoded by `de-op-stripe-customer`, writing the account row's `stripe-customer` and rewriting the view. `+stripe-checkout` (live branch): when the account's `stripe-customer` is blank, `+fetch` the customer request first, `set-stripe-customer`, and use the id; on a failure to create, refuse the checkout with `stripe answered <status>` as any other Stripe failure. `+credit-session` keeps storing the session's `customer` for a subscription, which is now the same id. Tests: the customer request's URL, auth and body; both session builders with and without a customer (the body carries `customer` only when given).

## 4. The failure and the dispute

`+serve-stripe-hook` handles two more types:

- `checkout.session.async_payment_failed`: `+fail-session |=(sid=@t (fiber ,[ok=? why=@t]))`: read the session, find the row by `sid` on the ship in its metadata, `mark-checkout` it `failed`; no ledger change; ring row `stripe.webhook`.
- `charge.dispute.created`: `+take-dispute |=(id=@t (fiber ,[ok=? why=@t]))`: `+dispute-request |=([base=@t key=@t id=@t] request:http)` GET `/v1/disputes/<id>` and `+read-dispute |=(body=@t (unit [id=@t intent=@t amount=@ud status=@t]))` (`payment_intent`, `amount` in cents, `status`); find the account whose `checkouts.json` holds a row with that `intent` (walk `/accounts`; `unknown payment` when none); write a `refund` row of `amount * 10.000` with `rail` `stripe`, `ref` `dispute-<id>`, `note` `dispute <status>` (deduped by the refund op, so a second delivery does nothing); then `reconcile` on that ship when it holds a lease, which sees the lower balance and lowers the cap or disables the key; ring row `stripe.dispute` with the ship and the amount. A balance that goes below zero blocks the proxy and the lease until a top-up covers it, which is the intent: the spent credit is gone and the customer is told so by the 402. `charge.dispute.closed` is noted in the ring with its `status` and changes nothing; the owner credits by hand when a dispute is won. `docs/payments.md` gains a Disputes paragraph saying exactly this.

`scripts/fake-stripe.py` gains `POST /stub/fail/<sid>` (marks the session `unpaid` with `payment_status` `unpaid` and POSTs `checkout.session.async_payment_failed`), `POST /stub/dispute/<sid>` (makes `dp_<n>` with `payment_intent` the session's, `amount` its total, `status` `needs_response`, POSTs `charge.dispute.created`), `GET /v1/disputes/<id>`, and `POST /v1/customers` answering `cus_<n>` with the metadata echoed; sessions created with `customer=` carry it. The Payments view's ring card already filters `stripe.`; the owner's account detail shows the Stripe customer id beside the subscription ids.
