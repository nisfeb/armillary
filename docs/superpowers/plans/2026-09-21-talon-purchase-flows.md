**The vendor.** Production is the ship `~nisfeb`, reached by every customer ship over ames; the customer's own ship holds the account. Nothing in Talon talks to the vendor directly: every call goes to `/apps/armillary` on the user's own ship over the cookie Talon holds. The desk on the customer's ship is installed from `~ricsul-bilwyt` like orrery and the calendar.

## 1. A default vendor, and the first run

`ArmillaryRepo` gains `const val DEFAULT_VENDOR = "~nisfeb"`. When the user adds the Armillary provider and the ship's `GET /api/account` answers with an empty `vendor`, the repo sets it with `PUT /api/vendor {"ship": DEFAULT_VENDOR}` before `ensureKey`, and the card shows "Vendor ~nisfeb" with a small Change affordance that opens a text field for another @p (a vendor is any ship running armillary; the user's own ship is a valid vendor for testing). A vendor change re-runs `ensureKey`. `ArmillaryAvailability.MISSING` on the probe keeps the existing text "Not on this ship. Install it from the Grubbery shell on your ship." and the card's buttons stay disabled; add one line under it: "Armillary is published by ~ricsul-bilwyt, the same as Orrery and the Calendar." The hello over ames is what opens the account, and the vendor refuses to take money from a ship that never spoke, so a card whose account shows `vendor` set but `balance` null for more than a minute says "Your ship is introducing itself to the vendor. Give it a moment and Refresh." rather than an error.

## 2. The sizes as buttons

The vendor's `GET /api/plans` lists top-up plans (`kind` `topup`, `price` equal to `credit`) and subscription plans. The `TopUpSheet` today lists every plan as one row and offers a custom amount. Change it to the shape the product wants: three fixed buttons across, labelled by their dollar amount ("$5", "$10", "$50"), taken from the top-up plans sorted by price, then "Another amount" with the dollars field, minimum `min_topup` from the vendor (the smallest top-up plan's price, else five dollars), then the rail choice as two toggles, Card and Bitcoin, Card selected by default, then Continue. A subscription plan is not a top-up: it stays on the card as the Subscribe button, labelled with the plan's name and price ("Subscribe: Talon Pro, $10 a month for $12 of credit"), and Bitcoin is not offered for it, since subscriptions are card only (the sheet's Bitcoin toggle is hidden when a subscription is being bought). When the vendor lists no top-up plans, show only the custom field. Every amount is shown with `money()`; the credit a size buys is the same as its price, so no second figure is shown.

## 3. Confirmation that money landed

`topUp` already watches the balance for two minutes after the URL opens. Surface it: the card shows "Waiting for your payment" with a small spinner while the watch runs, then "Paid: $X added" for ten seconds when the balance rises, and the plain balance after. When two minutes pass with no change the line says "No payment seen yet. If you paid, it arrives within a few minutes; Refresh to check." A bitcoin payment settles after a block, so for `rail` `btcpay` the watch runs for fifteen minutes instead of two and the waiting line says "Waiting for your bitcoin payment to confirm, usually ten to twenty minutes." The `checkouts` in the account view carry each row's `status` (`pending`, `processing`, `paid`, `failed`, `expired`, `refused`); read the newest row's status into the line: `processing` is "Payment seen, waiting for confirmation", `failed` is "The payment did not go through", `expired` is "The checkout expired before it was paid", `refused` shows the vendor's `note`. On desktop, the app window may be behind the browser: bring the card's message to the notifier too, one notification when the balance rises ("Armillary: $X added") through the existing `Notifier` interface, gated on the platform's notifier being real (the `Noop` one does nothing).

## 4. The failed-request path leads back to the card

When any AI feature fails with the 402 message from `outOfCredit`, the error surface that shows it (the catch-up dialog, `composerState.sendError`, the assistant's error line) offers a "Top up" action that navigates to Settings, AI, and opens the top-up sheet directly. Add a `pendingTopUp` flag on the AI settings state that the Armillary card reads once and clears. Where an error surface has no room for an action, the message alone stands.

## 5. Receipts and history

The card gains a "History" disclosure that lists the account's `ledger` rows from the view, newest first, up to fifty: date, kind (Credit, Charge, Refund), amount, and for a credit its rail ("card" or "bitcoin"); charges show the model and token counts on a second line when present. This is the customer's only receipt inside the app; Stripe and BTCPay send their own by email.
